# Reorder Desk: guide for shop owners

This guide takes about 10 minutes. You only need your phone.

## 1. Open Reorder Desk

Open the Reorder Desk link in Chrome on your phone. To keep it on your home screen like an app, tap the menu (three dots) and choose **Add to Home screen**.

Want to look around first? Tap **Try it with demo data**. Demo customers are made up, and WhatsApp will not open for them. Remove them later in **Settings**.

## 2. Set your shop name

Go to **Settings**, type your shop name, and tap **Save settings**. It goes into every message.

## 3. Add your products

Go to **Products**. For each product you sell again and again, enter how many days one unit lasts a normal customer.

Not sure? Use the calculator. A 3 kg bag of dog food (3000 g) at 150 g a day lasts 20 days.

Good products for reminders: pet food, cat litter, coffee, tea, baby wipes, diapers, formula, skincare, hair products, cooking gas, animal feed, water refills.

## 4. Add your orders

Go to **Orders**.

- **One at a time:** fill in the form after each sale.
- **Many at once:** if you keep orders in Excel or Google Sheets, save them as CSV and use **Import**. You need columns for phone, product and date.

Only add customers who agreed to hear from your shop.

## 5. Every morning, open Today

**Today** lists:

- **Thank-yous** for new customers
- **Reorder reminders** for customers about to run out
- **Win-backs** for customers who have gone quiet

Tap **Send on WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Check it, press send in WhatsApp, and come back. The customer drops off the list.

- **Copy** copies the message if you want to send it another way.
- **Skip** hides it without sending.
- **STOP** is for customers who asked you not to message them. They will never be suggested again.

## 6. Change the words

Go to **Messages**. Pick English or Kiswahili and edit the three messages. Keep the STOP line so customers can opt out.

## 7. Check your report

**Report** shows how many customers ordered more than once and how many orders came in after a reminder. Look at it each month.

## 8. Back up

Your data lives only on this phone, in this browser. Go to **Settings → Download backup** every week or two. If you change phones, open Reorder Desk on the new phone and use **Restore backup**.

Clearing your browser data deletes Reorder Desk data too, so keep a backup.
